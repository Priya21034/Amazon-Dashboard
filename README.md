# Project Name: Amazon Sales Dashboard

Key Metrics Visulialized:

* YTD Sales – Total revenue generated from the start of the year.
* QTD Sales – Revenue generated within the current quarter.
* YTD Products Sold – Total units sold since the start of the year.
* YTD Reviews – Customer feedback count collected this year.

* Sales Trends:- Sales by Month – Monthly revenue trends fortracking.
* Sales by Week – Weekly sales analysis.*
* Sales by Product Category – Breakdown Revenue OF different categories.

* Top Performers- Top 5 Products by YTD Sales – High-revenue products leading the market.
* Top 5 Products by Reviews – Most reviewed items, indicating customer engagement.

* Data Source: Amazon sales dataset (structured for analysis).
* Visualization Tools: Power BI (Bar charts, Column chart ,Matrix chart, slicers).
* DAX Calculations: Aggregated YTD, QTD metrics for trend analysis.
